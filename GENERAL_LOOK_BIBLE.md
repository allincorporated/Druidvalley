# General look bible (Pictish northern) — locked 18 Sep 2026

Source: user prose. No southern lime-spiked hair. No torcs.

## Hair
- Grown long; brushed back or tightly braided
- Held with carved bone or wooden pins (not lime-water spikes)

## Facial hair
- **No moustache-only look**
- Full **red / copper beard** (match Rhiney man reference + existing General art: lighter auburn/chestnut hair, distinctly red beard)
- Rugged middle-aged warrior face; amber eyes

## Face & skin
- **3kg silver neck chain from the start** (double-link; photo ref). NOT a torc. Upgrades via Pictish symbol carvings on terminals as power grows (plain → marked → fully carved)
- Optional permanent geometric tattoos or temporary woad blue markings

## Cloak (sagum)
- Thick heavy rectangular mantle, coarse dense local wool
- Multi-colour checked or striped weave (madder reds, weld yellows)
- Pinned over one shoulder with massive bronze penannular or dragonesque brooch
- Brooch: heavy, functional; red/yellow champlevé enamel matching armlets

## Tunic
- Loose knee-length flax linen or fine wool
- Cinch at waist; arms bare or sleeves rolled to show metalwork

## Arms (focal wealth) — placement LOCKED
- Silver chain always on; statement wealth ALSO on arms
- **Heavy armlet = UPPER ARM (bicep)** — Castle Newe / photo pair, ~1.5kg each, glass inserts. NOT on forearm.
- **Snake bangle = FOREARM (lower arm)** — bronze coil from photo, ~0.5kg. NOT on upper arm.
- Both can be worn together (heavy high + snake low). Never swap sites.
- Weight → deliberate, heavy posture
- Polished bronze + enamel flash on dark cloak

## Belt & lower
- Thick leather belt, heavy cast-bronze buckle
- Suspended: locally forged iron longsword in wood-leather scabbard, and/or carnyx mouthpiece for ceremony
- Braccae: form-fitting full-length woollen twill, cross-hatch/checked
- (High-status women: ankle-length wool dress, tablet-woven bands — later)

## Feet
- One-piece leather turnshoes (ox or deer hide), puckered stitch, raw-thong lace
- Lined with dried grass, moss, or raw sheep wool

## Gear mapping (game)
- Default look = this bible **with silver neck chain on**
- Slots: Weapon (belt sword later) | Cloak+brooch | Forearm snake bangle | Upper-arm heavy armlets | Neck chain (always equipped; upgrades = symbol carving tiers, not on/off)
- Codia: character turnarounds + ornament assets from user photos

## Life arc (visual progression)
The General is not one static portrait. Appearance tracks power:

1. **Young / weak / poor** — leaner frame; plain cloak; plain/lightly carved silver neck chain; little or no arm-metal; early thin red beard; reads humble
2. **Proven** — fuller red beard; snake bangle and/or one heavy armlet; better checked sagum + brooch; stronger build
3. **Old / strong / powerful** — older face; thick red/copper beard; Castle Newe heavy armlets; enamel brooch; dense cloak; heavy deliberate posture

Driven in-game by gear equipped + settlement / stats. Same man across stages (amber eyes; hair auburn→possible grey in elder). Silver neck chain worn from the start; elder power shows richer Pictish carvings on the same chain.
